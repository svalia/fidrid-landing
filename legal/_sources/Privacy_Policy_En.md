# FidRid — Privacy Policy

**Version 1.1 — effective 22 September 2026**

## 1. Who we are and what this policy covers

1.1. This Privacy Policy explains what data is processed when you use the **FidRid** mobile application for iOS (the "App"), for what purposes, on what legal basis, and what rights you have.

1.2. **Data controller:** Valentin Smolin, an individual, Russian Federation (the "Developer", "we").
**Contact for all privacy matters:** **taxers.59botanic@icloud.com**

1.3. This policy is written to meet the requirements of the EU General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR), the UK GDPR, the California Consumer Privacy Act as amended (CCPA/CPRA), and Apple App Store privacy requirements.

1.4. This policy covers the App only. It does not cover Telegram services, or any third-party website or app you may reach from the App.

## 2. Core principle: processing happens on your device

2.1. FidRid operates **no server infrastructure** for storing user data.

2.2. Your feed is assembled **on your device**. Channel and message content, your channel list, read states, folders, filters and favourites are held in the App's local database inside the protected iOS app container.

2.3. We **do not receive, store or disclose** the content of your messages or posts, and we do not build or sell reading statistics for individual channels.

## 3. What we process

| Category | Data | Where processed |
|---|---|---|
| Telegram authentication data | phone number, one-time confirmation code, cloud password (if 2FA is enabled) | sent directly to Telegram services; never stored by us |
| Session key | Telegram authorisation token | locally only, in secure device storage |
| Channel content and metadata | posts, media, channel names and avatars, dates, counters | locally only, on your device |
| Your settings | folders, filters, favourites, in-app read states, notification preferences | locally only, on your device |
| Device technical data | device model, iOS version, App version | sent to our analytics provider in anonymised form |
| Internal identifier | anonymised App installation identifier | sent to our analytics provider |
| Usage events | App launch, first launch, screen views, time spent in the App, anonymised interface interaction events (button taps, settings changes, completed actions) that do not identify which channel, post or feed they relate to | sent to our analytics provider |
| Aggregate counters | number of subscribed, enabled, muted and archived channels, number of feeds and favourite posts, state of App settings, with no channel, post or feed names or identifiers | sent to our analytics provider |
| IP address | not included in analytics events and not used to determine location; country is taken from the device region settings | not sent with events |

## 4. What we do not collect

The App does **not** collect or request:

- your name, email address or postal address;
- payment or banking data (the App is free and has no in-app purchases);
- precise or approximate location data;
- your device address book or contacts;
- biometric data;
- special categories of personal data (health, religious or political beliefs, and similar);
- the advertising identifier (IDFA) — the App shows no ads and does not request App Tracking Transparency permission;
- the content of your private correspondence — private chats and groups are never shown in the App.

We do **not sell or share** personal information for cross-context behavioural advertising, and we do not disclose data to advertisers, ad networks or data brokers. In the twelve months preceding the date of this policy we have sold or shared no personal information, including that of minors under 16.

## 5. Purposes and legal bases (GDPR Art. 6)

| Purpose | Data | Legal basis |
|---|---|---|
| Providing the App's functionality (sign-in, building the feed, notifications) | authentication data, session key, channel content, settings | Art. 6(1)(b) — performance of a contract |
| Diagnosing and fixing errors, keeping the App working | device technical data, anonymised identifier, usage events | Art. 6(1)(a) — consent; alternatively Art. 6(1)(f) — our legitimate interest in a functioning product |
| Improving the user experience and developing the product | anonymised usage events | Art. 6(1)(a) — consent |
| Sending push notifications | system push token | Art. 6(1)(a) — consent (iOS permission) |

Where processing relies on consent, you may withdraw it at any time; withdrawal does not affect the lawfulness of processing carried out before withdrawal.

## 6. Recipients and processors

6.1. **Telegram** (Telegram Messenger Inc. / Telegram FZ-LLC) — receives your authentication data and serves the channel content you request. Telegram acts as an **independent controller** for that processing under its own privacy policy, which we do not influence.

6.2. **Amplitude, Inc.** (United States) — product analytics provider, acting as our **processor**. It receives anonymised technical data and usage events. It never receives message or post content.

6.3. **Apple Inc.** — distributes the App through the App Store and delivers push notifications through APNs.

6.4. We disclose data to no one else, except where we are legally required to do so by a competent authority acting under applicable law.

## 7. International transfers

7.1. We are established in the Russian Federation, and our analytics provider is established in the United States. Neither country is the subject of a European Commission adequacy decision.

7.2. Only **anonymised** data reaches our analytics provider — data that does not allow you to be identified without additional information held separately.

7.3. Transfers to our analytics provider are made under the European Commission's **Standard Contractual Clauses** (GDPR Art. 46(2)(c)), supplemented by the provider's own technical safeguards. For UK transfers, the UK International Data Transfer Addendum applies.

7.4. You may request a copy of the relevant safeguards by writing to the contact address in section 1.

## 8. Retention

8.1. Data processed on your device is kept until you delete it in the App or delete the App itself. Deleting the App removes all local data.

8.2. Anonymised analytics data is retained by the analytics provider for no longer than **24 months** from collection.

8.3. Authentication data is never retained by us.

## 9. Your rights

9.1. Under the GDPR and the UK GDPR you have the right to: access your data; have it rectified; have it erased; restrict its processing; object to processing based on legitimate interests; receive your data in a portable format; withdraw consent; and not be subject to solely automated decision-making with legal or similarly significant effects (we carry out none).

9.2. Under the CCPA/CPRA, California residents have the right to know, delete and correct personal information, to opt out of sale or sharing (we do neither), to limit the use of sensitive personal information (we process none), and not to be discriminated against for exercising these rights.

9.3. How to exercise your rights in practice:

- **turn analytics off** — in the App's privacy settings;
- **end the session** — in the App settings or in the Telegram active sessions list;
- **delete all local data** — by deleting the App from your device;
- **make a request or withdraw consent** — by writing to taxers.59botanic@icloud.com.

9.4. We respond to requests **within one month** of receipt. That period may be extended by up to two further months where a request is complex; we will tell you if that happens. Requests are free of charge unless manifestly unfounded or excessive.

9.5. Because most data never leaves your device and analytics data is anonymised, we may be unable to identify you from the data we hold (GDPR Art. 11). In that case we will explain this and ask you to supply the App installation identifier shown in the App settings so that we can locate the relevant records.

9.6. You have the right to lodge a complaint with a supervisory authority — in the EU, the data protection authority of your country of residence, work or of the alleged infringement; in the UK, the Information Commissioner's Office (ico.org.uk).

## 10. Security

10.1. Communication with Telegram services uses Telegram's encrypted transport protocol.

10.2. The session key is stored in the secure iOS storage (Keychain); the local database sits in the sandboxed app container, access to which is restricted by the operating system.

10.3. Data sent to the analytics provider travels over HTTPS.

10.4. We hold no copies of your data, which removes the risk of a breach on our side.

## 11. Children

11.1. The App is not intended for anyone under **16** and we do not knowingly collect their data. If such data is identified, it will be deleted upon request from a parent or legal guardian.

## 12. Changes to this policy

12.1. We may update this policy. The current version is published on the App's website with its effective date.

12.2. Material changes will be brought to your attention the next time you open the App.
