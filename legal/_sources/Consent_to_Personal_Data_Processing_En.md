# FidRid — Consent to Personal Data Processing

**Version 1.1 — effective 22 September 2026**

By using the **FidRid** mobile application (the "App"), provided by **Valentin Smolin** (the "Developer"), I freely, specifically and in an informed and unambiguous manner, confirming that I am at least 16 years old and have legal capacity, give my consent to the processing of my personal data as described below.

This consent is given in accordance with Article 6(1)(a) and Article 7 of the EU General Data Protection Regulation and equivalent provisions of applicable data protection law.

## 1. Data covered by this consent

The following data may be processed while I use the App:

- device technical data (device model, iOS version, App version);
- an anonymised internal identifier of the App installation;
- usage data (App launch, first launch, screen views, time spent in the App, anonymised interface interaction events that do not identify which channel, post or feed they relate to);
- aggregate counters (number of channels, feeds and favourite posts, state of App settings), without channel, post or feed names or identifiers;
- country, taken from the device region settings; the IP address is not included in analytics events;
- the system push token — where I grant notification permission.

The following data is processed but is **not** covered by this consent, because a different legal basis applies:

- Telegram authentication data (phone number, one-time code, cloud password) and channel content — processed to perform the contract under Article 6(1)(b) GDPR. Authentication data is sent directly to Telegram services and is never stored by the Developer; channel content is processed **solely on my device**.

The App does not collect my name, email address, postal address, payment data, location data, address book, biometric data, or any special category of personal data.

## 2. Purposes of processing

My personal data is processed solely for the following purposes:

- analysing how the App is used;
- detecting and fixing errors;
- improving the user experience and developing the product;
- delivering push notifications (where permission is granted).

The App displays no advertising, does not use the advertising identifier (IDFA) and carries out no cross-app tracking.

## 3. Processing operations

The following operations may be performed: collection, recording, organisation, structuring, storage, adaptation, retrieval, use, anonymisation, restriction, erasure and destruction. Processing is carried out by automated and non-automated means. No solely automated decision-making producing legal or similarly significant effects is performed.

## 4. Analytics providers and international transfers

The App uses the third-party product analytics service **Amplitude, Inc.** (United States) as a processor. Distribution and push delivery are provided by **Apple Inc.**

Data is transferred to these services:

- in anonymised form;
- over HTTPS;
- without any message, post or private correspondence content.

I consent to the **international transfer** of the anonymised data described above to countries outside the European Economic Area and the United Kingdom, including the United States and the Russian Federation, which are not covered by a European Commission adequacy decision. Such transfers are protected by the Standard Contractual Clauses described in the Privacy Policy.

## 5. Duration

This consent remains valid until whichever occurs first:

- the purposes of processing are achieved; or
- I withdraw my consent.

## 6. Withdrawal of consent

I may withdraw this consent at any time, as easily as it was given, by:

- turning analytics off in the App's privacy settings;
- deleting the App from my device, which removes all local data;
- writing to **taxers.59botanic@icloud.com**.

Withdrawal does not affect the lawfulness of processing carried out on the basis of consent before its withdrawal.

## 7. Additional provisions

7.1. Giving this consent is voluntary and is not a condition of using the App's core functionality; refusing or withdrawing it has no detrimental effect on my use of the App other than the loss of features that depend on it.

7.2. Following withdrawal of consent, the Developer may continue processing where another legal basis under Article 6 GDPR applies, including compliance with a legal obligation or the establishment, exercise or defence of legal claims.

7.3. The processing of my data by Telegram services during sign-in and content retrieval is governed by the privacy policy of the owner of Telegram and is not covered by this consent.

7.4. I have the right to lodge a complaint with a supervisory authority. Full information on processing and on my rights is set out in the App's Privacy Policy.
